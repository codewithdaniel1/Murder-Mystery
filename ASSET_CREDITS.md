# Art and asset credits

All assets are bundled locally. No paid assets or external runtime services are required.

## Scanned estate materials and lighting

Eight Poly Haven materials include color, OpenGL normal and roughness maps at 1K. A 2K HDR sky provides natural lighting and background. All are CC0. Exact asset pages are listed in [material credits](public/materials/CREDITS.md).

## Scanned vegetation and furnishings

Poly Haven fern, shrubs, tree and wooden armchair, with 1K material maps. The tree was simplified from approximately 1.6 million triangles to about 54,000; instancing reuses shared geometry across the grounds. All are CC0. See [scenery credits](public/models/CREDITS.md).

## Characters and dog

Innerscene Harper and Jordan models use MakeHuman/MPFB CC0 assets and original Innerscene styling and animation. Both include idle and walking clips. Pip uses an original Innerscene dog model. All are CC0. See [character credits](public/models/people/CREDITS.md).

The civilian clothes are interim art and have not been verified as historically exact WWII costumes. Several NPCs share a base figure with different scale and fabric tint.

## Project-authored content

Estate architecture, decorative geometry, remaining props and vehicle, interface, case text, geometric rug pattern were authored for this project. No GTA assets, paid fonts or voice recordings are included.

## Recorded environmental and interaction audio

CC0 recordings replace the original synthesized effects: birds, wind, water, fire, footsteps, doors, paper, dog, engine, a nonverbal acknowledgment and a vintage telephone. Exact authors, sources, licenses and adaptations are in [audio credits](public/audio/CREDITS.md). Clips total approximately 1.56 MiB, with loop crossfades and conservative peak levels; measured metadata is in `public/audio/manifest.json`.
