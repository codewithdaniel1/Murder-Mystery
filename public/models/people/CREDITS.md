## Additional animated cast

The following Innerscene characters use MakeHuman/MPFB CC0 body, rig and skin assets and original Innerscene styling and authored animation. Their source pages explicitly dedicate the assets to CC0 1.0.

- sam.glb: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/locomotion-collection-sam-3d-person-animated-dc23800f
- maya.glb: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/locomotion-collection-maya-3d-person-animated-1499b2fd

Adaptations applied to Harper, Jordan, Sam and Maya: only `Idle_Standing` and `Walk_Relaxed` clips retained; original geometry, skin weights and skeleton unchanged. Body-color textures retain 2K resolution; remaining textures are capped at 1K. Opaque images are high-quality JPEG, transparent images remain PNG. All models remain self-contained GLB files. These are authored periodic motions, not motion-capture performances. Clothing is interim civilian art rather than a verified WWII wardrobe.
