# Scanned scenery models

Poly Haven assets, CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license

- fern.glb: https://polyhaven.com/a/fern_02
- shrub.glb: https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_02
- shrub-flowering.glb: https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_04
- tree.glb: https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_01
- armchair.glb: https://polyhaven.com/a/chinese_armchair

Original 1k glTF assets bundled to GLB. Tree geometry simplified offline with meshoptimizer, retaining UVs, normals and original material maps. Model meshes are instanced in the game to reuse geometry and textures.
